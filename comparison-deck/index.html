Slide 12 · AI Search Spotlight

Life Bible's killer feature

The prompt UX

LB
"The Bible has the answers to your life questions. Find them with Search." Live-suggestion list pre-populated with reframed questions ("Is the Bible reliable?", "Why does God allow suffering?"). Best onboarding to AI search I've seen.

GTF · How to beat it

GTF
GTF advantages over Life Bible's AI search:
  • Grounded answers — cite EGW + SDA Bible Commentary, not generic LLM output
  • Doctrinal guard rail — "Aligned with SDA Beliefs" badge already exists in devotional flow
  • Free — Life Bible AI search is behind Premium
  • Sabbath-aware — different prompts on Sabbath (rest-focused vs study-focused)
Recommended next build: mirror Life Bible's prompt-suggestion UX exactly — but seed it with SDA-distinctive questions ("What does Scripture teach about the state of the dead?", "Why do Adventists keep the seventh-day Sabbath?", "What is the Investigative Judgment?").
Slide 13 · Positioning Summary

Where GTF wins, ties, and trails

GTF wins

WIN
• Sabbath School integration (weekly lessons, Adventech sync)
• Sabbath-aware mode (auto theme + content shift)
• EGW writings as a first-class layer
• "Aligned with SDA Beliefs" doctrinal guard
• Church hierarchy + 96k+ church directory
• AI-generated cinematic videos (no competitor offers)
• Custom plan generator (any book, any duration)
• Private spiritual rings (no social pressure)

GTF ties

TIE
• Bible reader (good but lacks parallel panes)
• Highlights / bookmarks / notes
• Reading plans (curated quality > quantity)
• Audio Bible (TTS + ElevenLabs is competitive)
• Streaks / gamification
• Verse of the Day
• Daily devotional / reflection

GTF trails

GAP
• Translation count (curated vs YV's 2,500+)
• Topical Q&A library (Life Bible's TouchPoints)
• AI search prompt UX (Life Bible nailed it)
• Parallel-pane reading (Life Bible)
• Friend feed (YouVersion — but intentionally avoided)
• Giving / tithing entry (YouVersion)
• Events / church bulletins (YouVersion)
• Commentary breadth (Life Bible's Tyndale store)
Strategic recommendation

Don't try to out-feature YouVersion's social or Life Bible's commentary store. Double down on the Adventist moat (Sabbath School, EGW, doctrinal guard, church hierarchy) and selectively port the two killer competitor patterns: (1) Life Bible's AI search prompt UX grounded in EGW/SDA-BC, and (2) YouVersion's tappable cross-references in the reader.
