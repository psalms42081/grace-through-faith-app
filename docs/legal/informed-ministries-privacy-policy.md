# Informed Ministries App — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.2 — effective 10 November 2026**

Informed Ministries (ABN 39 741 036 497) ("we", "us", "our") operates the Informed Ministries mobile and web application (the "App"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we hold it, and your choices. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account: your name (as you choose to give it), email address, and a password we store only in hashed form.

**Home church and profile type.** If you choose a home church, or a profile type (Member, Student, Church Leader, or Just Exploring), we store that choice so the App can show relevant content. It is deleted with your account.

**Reports and blocks.** If you report a post or a person, we store the report: the reason, any comment, a snapshot of the reported content, your user id, and the reported user's id. We email that report to hello@informedministries.app. If you block someone, we store that block. You can review and unblock people from Profile → Settings → Blocked users.

**Your use of the App.** Reading-plan progress, devotional and Sabbath School progress, bookmarks, streaks, and the reflections, notes and journal entries you write. These are stored so the App can show them back to you.

**Small groups.** If you create or join a group: your display name, the groups you belong to, invite codes you use, the posts you make in a group, and whether you are currently in a video session. When you join a group you confirm you are 18 or over; we record that confirmation.

**AI features.** When you use Ask the Bible or the Study Tutor, the question you type (and, for the Tutor, the current lesson) is sent to OpenAI to generate a response. When you use the study generator or features such as Explain This Verse, the topic or passage is sent, not your identity. Generated study material may be stored and shown to other users who choose the same topic or passage; it is not linked to you. Ask the Bible questions we cache are deleted after 30 days. The Study Tutor conversation is held on your device for that session and is not stored on our servers.

**Technical information.** Device type, operating system, app version, approximate region derived from your connection, error reports, and basic usage events (for example that a screen was opened). We use this to keep the App working and to understand which features are used.

**Notification subscriptions.** If you turn on a Daily verse, Sabbath School, Reading plan, or Times of prayer reminder, we store the push endpoint for that browser or device, the time you chose, and your time zone. We use these only to send the reminder you asked for.

We do not collect payment information (the App is free), contacts, or photos. The App has no photo upload. If you search for a church, the place you type — or your device location, if you allow it — is used only to find nearby churches.

## 2. Why we collect it

We use personal information to:

- provide the App and remember where you are up to;
- run small groups and video sessions between members;
- answer questions you ask AI features;
- send you service messages (for example about changes to these terms or a security issue);
- keep the App secure, fix errors, and prevent misuse;
- understand, in aggregate, how the App is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not show advertising in the App.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the App, and only what each needs:

- The App's servers run on Render in Singapore. Render keeps service logs for its workspace plan: 7 days on Hobby, 14 days on Pro, and 30 days on Scale or Enterprise. We do not send those logs to another logging service.
- The database runs on Neon in Sydney, Australia.
- **OpenAI** receives the questions you type into Ask the Bible, the Study Tutor, study-guide replies, and devotional reflections, plus the verse or lesson text needed to answer them. We do not send OpenAI your name, email, or account id. OpenAI may keep those prompts for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
- **YouVersion** supplies Bible text for some translations. It receives the edition and chapter you open, not your name or email.
- **LiveKit** carries live group sessions. Audio and video pass through LiveKit during a session and are not recorded or stored by us. LiveKit receives your display name and a participant identity so you can join the room; it does not receive your email address.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** and **Zippopotam** turn a church search (a city, a US ZIP code, or a location you allowed) into a place on the map. The church-search cache on our server stores that place with no user id and expires after 30 days.
- **Expo** and **Firebase Cloud Messaging** deliver push notifications if you turn reminders on. They receive the push token and the reminder text, not your notes or email.
- **Resend** sends email we initiate to hello@informedministries.app. A new-account notice includes the new user's name and email. Problem reports, AI answer reports, and user reports include the reporter's email when we know it. When we know your email it is used as the reply-to address.
- **YouTube** plays ministry videos (including Watch this week and sermons). YouTube receives the video identifier; thumbnail images are loaded from YouTube. We do not send YouTube your account details. Some topic videos we produced are generated by HeyGen from our scripts and hosted on Cloudinary; HeyGen and Cloudinary do not receive your account details.
- **Other content providers** — Sabbath School lessons and some devotional content are fetched from third parties (for example Adventech, Our Daily Bread Ministries). We fetch content on your behalf; we do not send them your identity.

Some of these providers are based outside Australia, or may process data outside Australia. Where that happens we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle your information in a way consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

We may also disclose personal information where the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a person.

## 4. Group members

Other members of a group you join can see your display name, your posts in that group, and whether you are in a live session. They cannot see your email address, your private notes or reflections, or your activity outside the group. Please think of group content as shared with those members, and do not share personal information about other members outside the group.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account from Profile → Settings, the following are deleted immediately: your account, name, email, and hashed password; your home church choice and profile type; notes, highlights, and bookmarks; reading history and streaks; devotion and reading plans; Sabbath School progress and answers; prayers; push tokens and notification subscriptions; Bible-group posts; group discussion posts and replies; the blocks you created; and blocks other members had placed on you. Groups you host are closed. Posts you made in a group are removed; they are not kept as "Former member".

We keep these after deletion:

- Problem reports you sent are kept with your user id removed. AI answer reports are kept the same way: the text stays, and the user id is removed.
- Reports you made about a post or person are kept with your user id removed. Reports about you keep the content snapshot, with your user id removed.
- Server logs on Render are kept for Render's retention period (7 days on Hobby, 14 days on Pro, and 30 days on Scale or Enterprise) and are not copied to another logging service.
- OpenAI may keep prompts for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
- The church-search location cache has no user id and expires after 30 days.
- Records we must keep to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.
- Backups, which are overwritten in the ordinary course.

Notification subscriptions (the endpoint, the time you chose, and your time zone) are deleted when you turn the reminder off, and they are deleted with your account. Records of AI questions we cache are kept for no longer than 30 days.

## 6. Security

We protect personal information with measures including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access limited to those who need it, servers in Singapore, and a database in Sydney, Australia. No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction.** You can view and change most of your information in the App. For anything else, contact us and we will provide access or correct it within a reasonable time.
- **Deletion.** You can delete your account from Profile → Settings in the App, or follow the instructions at https://informedministries.app/delete-account. Can't sign in? Email hello@informedministries.app from your account email and we'll delete your account within 30 days.
- **Groups.** You can leave a group at any time; the host can remove members.
- **AI features.** You can use the App without ever using AI features; they only process what you type into them.
- **Complaints.** If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact us first. If we cannot resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change we will tell you in the App before it takes effect.
