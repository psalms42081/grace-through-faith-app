# Home reflections for review

Seeded copy from `components/home-v2/daily-reflections.ts`. One line is shown per local day. The same line returns after one full pass of the pool.

- Original set: the 7 lines already in the app (1–7).
- Written for this pool: 77 lines (8–84).

1. [original] Ephesians 2:8-9 — Grace is not a doctrine to be memorised but a Person to be embraced. Today, let Christ's unmerited favour reshape every anxious thought.

2. [original] Galatians 2:20 — The cross does not merely pardon the past; it empowers the present. Walk today in the strength of the One who conquered death.

3. [original] Exodus 20:8-11 — Sabbath rest is heaven's rhythm set in time — a weekly reminder that our worth is not in what we produce but in Whose we are.

4. [original] Romans 8:28 — Prayer is not convincing God to act; it is aligning our hearts with the One who is already working all things for good.

5. [original] 2 Corinthians 3:18 — When we behold Christ, we become like Him — not by straining to imitate, but by gazing until His character becomes our own.

6. [original] Hebrews 6:19 — Hope is not wishful thinking. It is the anchor of the soul, fastened to the promise of a God who cannot lie.

7. [original] 1 John 4:19 — Love your neighbour not because they deserve it, but because you have been loved beyond all deserving. Grace received becomes grace given.

8. [written] Psalm 34:18 — The Lord is near to the broken-hearted. Bring Him the weight you cannot name, and let His nearness be enough for this hour.

9. [written] 2 Corinthians 5:7 — Faith is taking God at His word when the path is still dark. One step in trust is enough light for today.

10. [written] Psalm 119:105 — The Word is a lamp, not a floodlight. It shows the next step, and that is the mercy.

11. [written] Deuteronomy 31:8 — Do not be afraid, for the Lord your God goes with you. His presence is the promise that outlasts the trouble.

12. [written] Lamentations 3:22-23 — Mercy is new this morning. Yesterday's failure does not get the last word over today's grace.

13. [written] Matthew 6:33 — Seek first the kingdom, and let the rest take its place. What you chase will shape the day.

14. [written] Psalm 46:10 — Be still, and know that He is God. Silence is not emptiness when He is the one who fills it.

15. [written] 1 Peter 5:7 — Cast your care on Him, because He cares for you. Anxiety was never meant to be carried alone.

16. [written] Nehemiah 8:10 — The joy of the Lord is strength for ordinary hours, not only for the days that feel triumphant.

17. [written] Proverbs 3:5-6 — Trust in the Lord with all your heart. Your own understanding is a poor map for a God who sees the end.

18. [written] Colossians 1:27 — Christ in you is the hope of glory. The life you need is not borrowed from tomorrow; it is His, given now.

19. [written] Colossians 3:13 — Forgive as you have been forgiven. The mercy you withhold is the mercy you have forgotten.

20. [written] Psalm 23:1 — The Lord is my shepherd. You are not required to be your own guide through the valley.

21. [written] Colossians 3:15 — Let the peace of Christ rule in your heart. Peace is a governor, not a mood you must manufacture.

22. [written] Psalm 46:1 — God is our refuge and strength, a very present help. He is not late to the trouble you are in.

23. [written] 1 John 1:7 — Walk in the light, as He is in the light. Hidden things lose their power when they are brought to Him.

24. [written] Exodus 14:14 — The Lord will fight for you; you need only be still. Some battles are won by refusing to panic.

25. [written] Romans 12:12 — Rejoice in hope, be patient in trouble, and keep praying. These three hold a day together.

26. [written] Hebrews 13:8 — Jesus is the same yesterday, today, and forever. Your changing feelings do not change His faithfulness.

27. [written] James 4:8 — Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Distance is not His preference.

28. [written] Numbers 6:24-26 — The Lord bless you and keep you. His face turned toward you is the good you cannot earn.

29. [written] John 15:4 — Abide in Me, He says. Fruit is the result of remaining, not of frantic effort.

30. [written] 2 Corinthians 12:9 — My grace is sufficient for you. The weakness you resent may be the place His strength is seen.

31. [written] Proverbs 18:10 — The name of the Lord is a strong tower. Run into it before you try to outrun the day.

32. [written] Philippians 1:6 — He who began a good work in you will carry it on. You are unfinished, and that is not failure.

33. [written] 1 Thessalonians 5:18 — Give thanks in all circumstances. Gratitude does not deny pain; it refuses to let pain be the only voice.

34. [written] Psalm 27:1 — The Lord is my light and my salvation. Fear loses its claim when He is the one you look to.

35. [written] 1 Corinthians 13:4 — Love is patient and kind. Today, let that be more than a reading; let it be how you answer someone.

36. [written] Matthew 11:28-29 — Come to Me, all who labour. The invitation is for the tired, and it still stands this morning.

37. [written] Philippians 4:13 — I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me. The 'all things' is His strength, not your reserve.

38. [written] Ephesians 4:32 — Be kind to one another, tender-hearted, forgiving. The church is built of such ordinary mercies.

39. [written] Isaiah 30:15 — In quietness and trust is your strength. Hurry is a poor counsellor.

40. [written] Psalm 147:3 — He heals the broken-hearted and binds up their wounds. Healing is His work, and He is not careless with yours.

41. [written] Matthew 5:16 — Let your light shine before others. A hidden kindness is still a light someone needed.

42. [written] Psalm 145:14 — The Lord upholds all who fall. Stumbling is not the end of His hold on you.

43. [written] Matthew 6:34 — Do not worry about tomorrow. Today has its own grace, and He has not given you tomorrow's yet.

44. [written] Nahum 1:7 — The Lord is good, a stronghold in the day of trouble. He knows those who take refuge in Him.

45. [written] Matthew 5:9 — Blessed are the peacemakers. Peace-making begins with the words you choose before the conflict grows.

46. [written] John 3:16 — God so loved the world that He gave His Son. Love, in Him, is a gift before it is a demand.

47. [written] Joshua 1:9 — Be strong and courageous. The command comes with a promise: He will not leave you.

48. [written] Isaiah 40:31 — Those who wait on the Lord renew their strength. Waiting is not wasting when He is the one you wait for.

49. [written] Galatians 5:22 — The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, and peace. These grow where His life is welcomed, not where you strain.

50. [written] 1 Peter 5:6 — Humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God. Humility is safe because His hand is the one over you.

51. [written] Jeremiah 31:3 — I have loved you with an everlasting love. His affection for you did not start when you improved.

52. [written] Galatians 6:9 — Let us not grow weary in doing good. The harvest comes in its season, and today is part of the sowing.

53. [written] Psalm 51:10 — Create in me a clean heart. Confession is how a day begins again without pretending.

54. [written] John 14:6 — Jesus said, I am the way, the truth, and the life. Direction, honesty, and life are a Person.

55. [written] Philippians 4:7 — The peace of God will guard your heart and mind. Peace is a sentry, not a feeling you must hold in place.

56. [written] Psalm 34:8 — Taste and see that the Lord is good. Faith deepens when you take Him at His word in something small today.

57. [written] Galatians 6:2 — Bear one another's burdens. Someone near you is carrying more than they have said.

58. [written] Zephaniah 3:17 — The Lord your God is with you, the Mighty One who saves. He rejoices over you, even on an ordinary day.

59. [written] Matthew 7:7 — Ask, and it will be given. Prayer is welcome in the throne room, not an interruption.

60. [written] Micah 6:8 — He has shown you what is good: to do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God.

61. [written] Lamentations 3:24 — The Lord is my portion. When other things are thin, He is still the inheritance.

62. [written] Hebrews 3:13 — Encourage one another daily. A true word, spoken in time, can keep a heart from hardening.

63. [written] 1 John 1:5 — God is light, and in Him is no darkness at all. Bring the day into that light before you edit it.

64. [written] Hebrews 13:5 — I will never leave you nor forsake you. Absence is not one of His ways with His own.

65. [written] Luke 6:45 — The mouth speaks what the heart is full of. Guard the spring, and the words will follow.

66. [written] Proverbs 16:3 — Commit your work to the Lord, and your plans will be established. Offer the day before you manage it.

67. [written] Ephesians 2:10 — You are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works. You were made for more than survival.

68. [written] Psalm 4:3 — The Lord hears when I call. Prayer is not sent into silence.

69. [written] Psalm 146:8 — The Lord opens the eyes of the blind. Ask Him to show you what you have been walking past.

70. [written] Philippians 4:4 — Rejoice always. Joy is a decision to remember who holds the day, not a denial of what the day holds.

71. [written] Psalm 23:2 — He leads me beside still waters. Rest is on the path, not only at the end of it.

72. [written] 1 John 1:9 — If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive. Honesty is met with mercy, not with a lecture.

73. [written] Psalm 29:11 — The Lord gives strength to His people. Ask for the strength of this hour, not for a lifetime in advance.

74. [written] Matthew 5:6 — Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness. The appetite itself is a gift from Him.

75. [written] Matthew 28:20 — I am with you always, to the end of the age. The promise covers this morning, not only the last day.

76. [written] Proverbs 15:1 — A gentle answer turns away wrath. You can lower the temperature of a room with one unhurried sentence.

77. [written] Psalm 145:18 — The Lord is close to all who call on Him in truth. Pretending is not required for prayer.

78. [written] Romans 8:37 — We are more than conquerors through Him who loved us. The victory is His love, not your winning streak.

79. [written] Exodus 20:8 — Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. Rest tells the truth that God can hold the world without your labour.

80. [written] Romans 8:26 — The Spirit helps us in our weakness. When you do not know what to pray, He is not waiting for better words.

81. [written] Hebrews 12:2 — Fix your eyes on Jesus, the author and finisher of faith. Look up before you look around.

82. [written] Psalm 121:8 — The Lord will keep your going out and your coming in. Ordinary doors are under His care.

83. [written] Matthew 5:44 — Love your enemies, and pray for those who mistreat you. Prayer is how enmity loses its grip on you.

84. [written] Ephesians 3:20 — God is able to do far more than we ask or imagine. Leave room in the day for a goodness you did not plan.
