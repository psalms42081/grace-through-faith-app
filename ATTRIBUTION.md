# Attribution

## STEP Bible tagged text (word maps)

Old and New Testament word-to-Strong's maps are ingested from
[STEP Bible Data](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data)
(TAHOT — Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT; TAGNT — Translators Amalgamated Greek NT).

Copyright Tyndale House Cambridge / [STEP Bible](https://www.stepbible.org).
Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit: STEP Bible, linked to https://www.stepbible.org.

There is no KJV-tagged alignment file in that repository (the ESV tags are
CC BY-NC and are not used). English glosses come from STEP's TAHOT/TAGNT
translation column; TAGNT words are limited to those marked as present in the
KJV / Textus Receptus tradition.

## OpenScriptures lexicon markup

Hebrew and Greek Strong's dictionary rows in `data/strongs.json` / `strong_entry`
use the XML encoding of
[OpenScriptures Strong's](https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs) and
[OpenScriptures HebrewLexicon](https://github.com/openscriptures/HebrewLexicon),
typically licensed CC BY 4.0 for the markup.
