ROLE

---

PROJECT CONTEXT

The application is a Spiritual Formation platform for Seventh-day Adventists.

The app already includes the following systems:

• Bible reading
• Deep Study (Text → Context → Insight → Transformation)
• Prayer journal
• Sabbath Experience (time-based spiritual environment)
• Sabbath School (Adventech integration)
• Prophecy Explorer
• Kids Mode with stories, quizzes, memory verses, rewards
• Spiritual Growth Map (5 dimensions of growth)
• Groups and discussion features

The product direction is not a Bible utility, but a guided spiritual formation journey.

The core product loop should feel like:

Read → Reflect → Pray → Practice → Grow
