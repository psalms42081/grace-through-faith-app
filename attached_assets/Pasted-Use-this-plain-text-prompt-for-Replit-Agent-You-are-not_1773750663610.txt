Use this plain-text prompt for Replit Agent:

You are not building new features from scratch. You are doing a product focus and hierarchy pass on an app that is already 85–90% built.

Read the current code first. Audit what already exists. Do not duplicate systems. Do not create parallel versions of features. Do not add feature creep.

Goal: Refocus the app so beta clearly leads with its strongest, most mission-aligned pillars: Scripture Prayer Sabbath School Study Family / Kids Church connection

The problem to solve: The app is powerful, but it risks feeling too broad and too feature-heavy. We do not want to remove the value of the app. We want to make the hierarchy clearer so it feels focused, calm, and premium.

Core product direction: This app should feel like a digital discipleship and church-connection companion. It should not feel like:

an “everything SDA” app

an online-only church substitute

a media aggregator

an experimental feature bundle


Do not rebuild the app. Do not invent new major systems. Do not remove good features unnecessarily. Do not make broad speculative changes.

What to do:

1. Audit the current app structure Before changing anything, identify what currently exists in these categories:



Read

Study

Pray

Sabbath School

Family / Kids

Connect / church / groups

Maps / timeline / prophecy

Media / speakers / broadcasts / extras

AI / helper / advanced tools


2. Reorganize emphasis, not everything Your job is to elevate, demote, hide, or simplify — not destroy.



Treat features in these three buckets:

A. Must lead These should be the clearest visible pillars across home, onboarding, section ordering, and key messaging:

Read

Study

Pray

Sabbath School

Family / Kids

Connect to church / community


B. Keep, but secondary These stay in the app, but should not dominate the first impression:

Maps

Timeline

Prophecy Explorer

Formation tracks / deeper growth systems

Notes / saved content / journaling depth

Historic context / commentary / advanced study tools


C. Hide for beta or reduce emphasis Only if already present and currently distracting:

anything that feels like a church replacement

anything that behaves like a social network for its own sake

extra media ecosystem layers that do not clearly support discipleship

AI-first framing that makes the app sound like a bot instead of a ministry tool

broad speaker/radio/broadcast emphasis if it clutters the product identity


3. Tighten home and top-level hierarchy Review the home screen and top-level structure. Without rebuilding the app, make sure the strongest user-facing hierarchy is:



Primary:

Bible reading

prayer

Sabbath School

study

family / kids

connect


Secondary:

maps

prophecy

timeline

deeper study layers


Do not just add more cards. Do not create clutter. Prefer simplifying or reordering existing sections.

4. Clean up wording and product framing Where needed, refine wording so the app sounds like:



a Scripture-centered formation tool

a prayer and study companion

a Sabbath School and family discipleship platform

a bridge into real church connection


It should not sound like:

an app that replaces church

a digital-only spirituality product

a feature list


5. Reduce overwhelm Look for screens, tabs, or entry points where users are being shown too much at once. Make the smallest changes needed to reduce overload:



better ordering

simpler labels

fewer competing hero elements

cleaner grouping

better section priority


Do not gut the app. Do not remove depth. Just make the depth feel secondary to the core.

6. Preserve what already works Keep and respect:



study systems

prayer systems

Sabbath School

family features

maps direction

church connection direction

existing routing and architecture where possible


Do not touch things unnecessarily just because they are there.

7. Beta mindset This pass should make the app feel:



more focused

less noisy

easier to understand in 20 seconds

more aligned with discipleship and local church life

more premium


8. Deliverable After making changes, report back with:



exact files changed

which features were elevated

which features were demoted or visually de-emphasized

which things were hidden or simplified for beta

what was deliberately left unchanged


Important: Do this as one careful refinement pass. Do not add new large systems. Do not rebuild from zero. Do not overcomplicate. The goal is product clarity, not feature expansion.
