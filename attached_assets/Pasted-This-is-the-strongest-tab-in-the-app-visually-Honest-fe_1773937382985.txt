This is the strongest tab in the app visually. Honest feedback:

**Overall**

The image selection here is exceptional. The ancient scrolls for Study Scripture, cathedral light rays for Sabbath School, the forest path for Learning Paths, the antique map for Study Tools, the Three Angels painting for Essentials, and the cross at sunset for Spiritual Themes — every image is thematically matched and tonally consistent. This looks like a premium product.

**What's working perfectly**

The subtitle pattern is consistent and effective throughout — each card gives you the category name plus a comma-separated list of what's inside. "Quick Read, Guided Study, Deep Dive" tells you exactly what to expect. This is good information architecture.

The card heights are varied naturally by content, which breaks up the monotony without feeling inconsistent. The Three Angels card in image 2 is particularly striking — the Renaissance painting works beautifully at this scale.

**One real issue: "Essentials" is underselling itself**

The subtitle reads "Three Angels' Messages, Health Message" — only two items, and neither one signals to a new user what this section actually is. Someone unfamiliar with SDA terminology won't know what "Three Angels' Messages" means. Consider "Core Adventist beliefs and distinctive doctrines" as the subtitle, or add "28 Fundamental Beliefs" to the list since that's likely also in this section. The image (Three Angels) is perfect for this card — the text just needs to match its weight.

**"Spiritual Themes" card**

The cross-at-sunset image is beautiful but it's the most generic religious image in the set — it could belong to any Christian app. Every other card has imagery that's specific and distinctive. Consider swapping it for something that better represents "themes" — perhaps an image evoking reflection or multiple threads coming together.

**Structural observation**

Six cards across two screens means the Study tab has the most depth of any tab. That's appropriate — Study is the core product. But a first-time user scrolling through will see six equal-weight entry points with no signal about where to start. A subtle "Start here" indicator or a different visual treatment on "Study Scripture" (the primary entry point) would help with orientation without redesigning the tab.

The Study tab is genuinely portfolio-quality work. The Essentials subtitle is the only text fix needed. Send the remaining screens when ready.
