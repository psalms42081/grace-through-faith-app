Lesson sections use 6 types: anchor, explain, integrate, practice, reflection, assessment.

Goal:
Complete and polish the first 7 belief modules of the “28 Beliefs Deep Dive” track as a structured theological course for intelligent lay believers.

Non-negotiables:
- All content must be ORIGINAL.
- Do NOT copy, paraphrase, or mirror General Conference quarterly text.
- Align with Adventist theology by using:
  - the official Fundamental Belief statement as a doctrinal boundary
  - Scripture-first reasoning
- Tone: clear, reverent, lay-friendly, not academic.
- Every module must have explicit internal consistency and progression.

Work Scope:
For each of Beliefs 1–7, create 4 fully-seeded lessons (28 total lessons), each with all 6 sections plus assessments:

Belief Module Lesson Pattern:
Lesson 1 — Biblical Foundations
Lesson 2 — Doctrinal Formulation (what Adventists affirm, key terms)
Lesson 3 — Practical Implications (life, worship, identity)
Lesson 4 — Misunderstandings & Challenges (common errors corrected)

For EACH lesson:
1) Anchor section:
   - Provide key Bible passages (3–6 references)
   - Include a short reading instruction (what to look for)

2) Explain section:
   - Original explanation of the belief, clear and structured

3) Integrate section:
   - Show how this belief connects to the Great Controversy theme and Adventist identity
   - Use Scripture, not quotations from copyrighted curriculum
   - You may link externally to public sources (e.g., Adventist.org belief page) but do not paste their text.

4) Practice section:
   - Provide 2–3 concrete actions (small, doable, not vague)
   - Examples: “Write a 5 sentence summary”, “Pray through Psalm X”, “Discuss with family using prompt Y”

5) Reflection section:
   - 2–3 thoughtful journal prompts tied to the lesson content

6) Assessment section:
   - 5 multiple-choice questions per lesson
   - Mix:
     - 60% recall
     - 40% conceptual clarity
   - Include correct answer index and short explanation

- Ensure lessons unlock sequentially as the current system enforces.

Acceptance Criteria:
