I agree — Live Fellowship is the right name. It communicates the purpose immediately and fits Adventist culture well. It also scales from 2 people praying together to thousands attending a conference without needing different terminology.

Let’s define it clearly so the feature becomes structured and understandable in the app.


---

Live Fellowship Structure

Instead of the feature feeling like a random “group chat”, structure it like this:

Live Fellowship
│
├ Live Now
│   Join active prayer rooms, studies, or conferences
│
├ Small Groups
│   Private invite-based groups
│
├ Upcoming Events
│   Scheduled Bible studies or conferences
│
└ My Groups
    Groups you host or belong to

This makes it obvious what the feature does.

Public Events (Conferences)

Public events should appear under Live Now.

Example:

Live Fellowship

LIVE NOW
Prophecy Conference
Hosted by Amazing Facts

[Join Live Session]

Upcoming
Global Sabbath School
Saturday 9:00 AM

This is how you allow large conferences to be attended worldwide.


---

Important Moderator Controls

Since this allows global participation, hosts should be able to:

• mute participants
• remove users
• control who can speak
• end session

LiveKit supports these.


---

Why This Feature Matters

Most Bible apps are content platforms.

Live Fellowship turns your app into:

Scripture + Community + Teaching

That combination is rare.


---

What I Recommend Next

Before adding more complexity, finish polishing three things:

1. Name the feature “Live Fellowship” everywhere


2. Add a visible “Live” tab in groups


3. Create a simple “Live Now” section



Once those are clean, the feature will make much more sense to users.
