Delete your Informed Ministries account

This page is for the Informed Ministries app. You can delete your account at any time. Deletion is permanent.

How to delete

  1. Sign in to Informed Ministries in the app or at informedministries.app.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the warning, and confirm.

Can't sign in? Email hello@informedministries.app from your account email and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Deleted immediately

  • your account, name, email, and hashed password
  • your home church choice and profile type
  • notes, highlights, and bookmarks
  • reading history and streaks
  • devotion and reading plans
  • Sabbath School progress and answers
  • prayers
  • push tokens and notification subscriptions
  • Bible-group posts
  • group discussion posts and replies
  • blocks you created, and blocks other members had placed on you

Groups you host are closed. Posts you made are removed; they are not kept as "Former member".

What we keep

  • Problem reports and AI answer reports keep their text, with your user id removed.
  • Reports you made about a post or person are kept with your user id removed. Reports about you keep the content snapshot, with your user id removed.
  • Server logs on Render are kept for Render's retention period: 7 days on Hobby, 14 days on Pro, and 30 days on Scale or Enterprise. We do not copy those logs to another logging service.
  • OpenAI may keep prompts for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • The church-search location cache has no user id and expires after 30 days.
  • Records we must keep to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute.
  • Backups, which are overwritten in the ordinary course, as described in the Privacy Policy.
