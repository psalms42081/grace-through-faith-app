Delete your Informed Ministries account

You can delete your account at any time. Deletion is permanent.

How to delete

  • Sign in to Informed Ministries in the app or at informedministries.app.
  • Open Profile, then Settings.
  • Tap Delete account, read the warning, and confirm.

If you cannot sign in, email hello@informedministries.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm it is you before deleting.

What is removed

When you delete your account we remove:

  • your login (name, email, and hashed password)
  • notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading history, and streaks
  • devotion, reading-plan, and Sabbath School progress, including journal entries
  • prayer requests you created, notification subscriptions, and device tokens
  • small-group memberships; groups you host are closed, and posts you made are removed

What we keep

  • Scripture, devotionals, churches, and other shared catalog content (these are not your personal data)
  • Problem reports you sent, with your user id removed where the system allows it
  • Records we must keep to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute
  • Backups, which are overwritten in the ordinary course, as described in the Privacy Policy
