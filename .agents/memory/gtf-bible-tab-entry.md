---
name: Bible tab entry
description: Product rule for the passage opened when a member taps the Bible tab.
---

The Bible tab should open Scripture immediately: resume the latest chapter for a signed-in reader, or open the chapter containing today’s verse for a new or guest reader. Keep the full book browser available as a secondary reader control.

**Why:** GTF is Scripture-first. Opening a book directory before showing any Bible text makes the app feel less direct than the YouVersion benchmark and adds friction before reading.

**How to apply:** Preserve this behavior when changing tab navigation, reading history, daily verses, or the Bible reader. The resumed passage must use the member’s currently selected translation.
