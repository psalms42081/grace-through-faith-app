---
name: Sabbath School quarter colors
description: Product rule for applying official quarterly colors across the canonical adult Sabbath School experience.
---

Use the official quarterly color supplied by the lesson feed across canonical adult Sabbath School surfaces, including Home entry, quarter landing, archive, and daily lesson accents. Keep the established teal when the feed color is missing or invalid.

**Why:** The user wants the app to follow the visual identity of each printed quarterly rather than remain permanently green. Feed colors can be too bright for white text, so accessibility takes precedence over exact reproduction.

**How to apply:** Validate feed colors, darken bright colors enough for readable white text, and fall back to teal. Keep coral for the Today marker and green for completion/success; those are semantic states, not quarterly branding. Do not apply this rule to Kids Sabbath School or legacy lesson systems.
