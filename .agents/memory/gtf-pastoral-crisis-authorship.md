---
name: Pastoral crisis authorship
description: Human-authorship boundary for clinical, crisis, grief, anxiety, and addiction guidance.
---

The agent may design and implement the region-aware crisis-resource frame, placement, data contracts, accessibility, and tests. A designated human reviewer must personally author and approve every sentence of pastoral and clinical guidance shown in the deepest sensitive-topic content.

**Why:** Guidance at the intersection of SDA theology and trauma-informed practice requires accountable human judgment and provenance. It must not be silently generated or treated as ordinary product copy.

**How to apply:** Keep resource plumbing and authored content separate. Provide clearly identified copy slots and a review surface, do not invent final guidance text, and preserve human authorship metadata when the approved copy is wired into Grief, Anxiety, Addiction, or related crisis surfaces.
