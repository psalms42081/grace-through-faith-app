Devotions uses one landing in this order: Continue Today, Daily Readings, Devotional Series, Reading Plans, Build Your Own, and Your Shelf. It gathers reading plans, Our Daily Bread, Ellen G. White daily readings, and devotional series, while Sabbath School remains a separate teal practice and route chain.

**Why:** The user confirmed that Sabbath School day must remain separate because combining it with Devotions would blur two distinct Adventist practices. The user also requires device review before any live tab or canonical-route swap.

**How to apply:** Build and review Devotions changes through hidden preview routes first. Do not rename the live Plans tab or replace public devotional routes until explicit device approval. After approval, use `/devotions` as canonical with compatibility from `/plans` and `/devotionals`.
