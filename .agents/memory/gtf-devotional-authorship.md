---
name: Devotional authorship boundary
description: Rules separating human-authored catalog devotionals from AI study assistance, including legacy enrollment protection.
---

Only editorially reviewed, human-curated devotional series may appear in a discovery catalog. AI can support Bible study tools, tutoring, topic studies, verse explanations, and reflections, but must not author content presented as a devotional.

**Why:** A devotional carries pastoral and authorship expectations that are different from interactive study assistance.

**How to apply:** New catalog series require explicit human-curated provenance. AI-generated and legacy-unclassified series stay out of catalogs without deletion. Never use catalog eligibility predicates in enrollment, today, day-reader, completion, progress, or shelf paths: people who already started a custom or AI plan must be able to finish and retain their history. Catalog recovery must be an allowlisted, idempotent data operation that repairs approved content without deleting plans, enrollments, or progress.
